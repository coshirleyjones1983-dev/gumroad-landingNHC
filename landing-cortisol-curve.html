Two shapes, one hormone

What does your cortisol curve look like right now?

Cortisol is meant to be high in the morning and taper off by night. Tap below to compare that shape with the pattern most people actually live with.

Morning Afternoon Night

The target shape: cortisol high in the morning to get you moving, easing gradually so you can wind down by night. The pattern this book addresses: low morning energy (wired but tired), a dip that hits mid-afternoon, and a spike right when you're trying to sleep (evening overstimulation).

Named protocols for each pattern

Three ways the curve loses its shape

  • 01

    Wired but tired

    Mornings that never quite wake up, no matter how much sleep you got.

  • 02

    The afternoon crash

    A hard drop that hits at the same time, every day, whether you've eaten or not.

  • 03

    Evening overstimulation

    Suddenly alert right when you're trying to wind down for the night.
